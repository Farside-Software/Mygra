## Description (3075/4000)

Keep a clear, private record of your migraines.

Mygra is a calm, private migraine journal. Log an attack in seconds, attach how you slept and what the weather was doing, and see your history take shape. Everything stays on your device and in your own iCloud, and connects with Apple Health for the full picture.

FREE TO DOWNLOAD, CORE LOGGING FREE FOREVER
• Record start and end times, pain and stress on a 0–10 scale, triggers from a curated list (or your own), medications, foods, and notes.
• Follow an attack as it happens with a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Adjust pain with - and + or tap End without opening the app.
• Health and weather are attached automatically, so each entry records how you slept, what you drank, and the conditions outside.
• A dashboard that shows this month against last month, a 30-day calendar strip, your most-logged triggers and medications, and when your migraines tend to start.
• A calendar colored by severity, plus a searchable history grouped by month, with pins, tags, and filters.
• Completed migraines are written back to Apple Health as headaches.

EVERYWHERE YOU ARE
• iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro.
• Built for iPhone Duo: two-column detail pages, a calendar beside the day's list, and a Settings split view when unfolded.
• Apple Watch app and complication, and a Home Screen widget with days since your last migraine.
• Siri and Shortcuts: ask when your last migraine was or how many you have had this month, log a medication or a trigger, and start or end a migraine by voice. Water and caffeine actions too.

MYGRA PLUS
Add the insight layer when you want it:
• Migraine Assistant: chat with on-device Apple Intelligence that can look up your actual entries and streams its answers as it writes. Every word stays on your device.
• Quick Bits and trigger analysis: trends across triggers, sleep, hydration, and weather.
• Weather alerts: a notification when barometric pressure or the weather changes sharply.
• Cycle Insights: see how your attacks line up with your menstrual cycle.
• Doctor-ready PDF reports and CSV export of your full history.

Mygra Plus is $2.99 a month, $14.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $29.99 once, yours forever. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Settings. Family Sharing included. Bought Mygra before 2.0? Plus is yours, free, forever.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data lives on your device and in your personal iCloud. Mygra has no accounts, no servers, and no tracking. AI analysis never leaves your device.

Whether you live with chronic migraines or the occasional bad headache, Mygra helps you keep a clear record so you can talk it through with your care team.

Mygra is a journal, not a medical device. Insights are for informational purposes only and are not medical advice. The Migraine Assistant requires Apple Intelligence. Available in English, Spanish, French (Canada), and Japanese.
